# Project Minecraft (NeoForge 1.21.1)

Project Moon in Minecraft: Roland's gloves and Furioso, three E.G.O weapons from Limbus Company, and the Black Silence's reception in the Library from Library of Ruina. Everything is in the Project Minecraft creative tab.

These used to be part of [Random Slop](https://github.com/Vihellnisch/Effect-Slop). Items from old worlds carry over: the old `effectslop:` ids are aliased to `projectminecraft:`.

## The Black Silence (boss)

A **Library Invitation** turns up now and then in ancient city, end city, stronghold, woodland mansion and ominous trial chests. Hold it up and the pages take you to **the Library**, your own reception, where Roland waits at his desk. Use it again there to go home. Lose, and you wake at home with everything you carried and the invitation still in hand; win, and it's spent. Fought after the reception in Library of Ruina:

1. **The Black Silence**: his nine workshop weapons one page at a time (Atelier Logic's pistols and shotgun, Zelkova's axe and mace, Allas's lance, Durandal, Old Boys' hammer, Wheels' greatsword, Ranga's gauntlets and knife, Crystal Atelier's short swords, Mook's katana). Once all nine are spent: **Furioso**.
2. **Distorted**: he breaks into a crawling beast of flesh, eye holes, maws and black tendrils, and the floor gives way to a factory under a tower of pipes. Claws, tail, a lunging bite, a sinew rope that drags you in, tendrils bursting from the floor, and **Suffocating Haze**: up to three stacks, each choking you with something every few seconds. Four Smouldering Byproducts fight with him, and crawl back once.
3. **Roland and Angelica**: the distortion settles into smoke around him, his weapons black with it, and Angelica forms out of white mist behind him. **Soul Link**: bring one down and they come back a few seconds later, a link fewer, unless you bring the other down while they're still mist. With no links left, both stagger. Their combos, Dark Bond and Ashen Bond, and the **Waltz in Black and White**, which knocks the weapon out of your hand.
4. **The Black Silence and his weapons**: memories of guests he fought (each with its own colour), and every few seconds three of his weapons rise behind him. Break them before the next scene, or they're nailed into the floor, and every nailed one adds two blows to his next Furioso.
5. **Images of the Bygones**: he crouches in his smoke, untouchable, while four images of old guests fight for him, then rises and **Screams**.

He drops the **Black Silence** gloves and his **Perception-Blocking Mask**: a plain black square plate worn over the face. While you wear it, hostile creatures don't notice you, not even an enderman meeting your eyes; strike one and that one sees you and comes for you. Leave the Library mid-fight and he goes back to his desk. Skins by the user.

Invitations turn up in woodland mansion chests (12%), ancient city and end city treasure chests (6%), stronghold chests (5%) and ominous trial vaults (4%).

## The Claw (boss)

Roland also drops **the Black Silence's Book**. Open it (not in creative, not in the Library) and the Head notices: **Baral, the Claw**, tears through space with Serum W and the fight starts right there. He never breaks a block.

- **Trail** (two claw swipes) and **Extirpation** (a short blink, a thrust and a yank back).
- **Serum R**: an orange line marks his path, then a 30-block charge that burns.
- **Serum W**: blue marks on you and your pets; he blinks to each and cuts. Break line of sight to lose a mark.
- **Serum K**: a green orb, and 15% of his health back after 4 s. Hit him for 10% of his health while he holds a serum and he's **groggy** (kneels for 6 s, takes 50% more).
- **No-Sell**: the first hit of 30+ in the fight does nothing.
- **Through every floor** (at half health, and after every Tri-serum): a side-on cinematic, the way Library of Ruina shows it. He has you by the throat and rushes you across one place after another (three dimensions from the config, mod dimensions too), a slash at the end of each, then through a void and back for the last cut. The places are real: a slab of each dimension is copied and shown as the backdrop, so nobody actually leaves, no loading screens. The slashes before the last one can't kill. Anyone watching sees you held in a blue shimmer.
- **Tri-serum Cocktail** (below a quarter): untouchable while it takes, then five dashes through you that ignore armour and heal him.

Bring him down and he doesn't die: his claw and serums break, the book you carried is torn into a plain book, and he warps out. He drops the **Broken Claw**, **Broken Serum K/R/W** and his **mask** (a trophy you can wear). Die and he takes the book and goes.

Mend them at a crafting table: Broken Claw + 2 netherite scrap + 2 iron = **Claw Arm**; each broken serum + a glass bottle + a golden apple (K), blaze powder (R) or ender pearl (W) = **Serum K/R/W**; Claw Arm + the three serums = the **Handmade Claw**, worn over your forearm with its three vials on the back of the hand. With it in hand, **B** picks a serum (tap: next one, hold: the wheel) and right-click injects it:
- **K**: green aura, life steal and regeneration (10 s).
- **R**: red aura, strength, resistance and speed, and your hits burn (10 s).
- **W**: an 8-block blink that cuts whatever you pass; the next hit is a big slash (3 s cooldown).
- **Tri-serum**: all three, stronger, for 20 s. Right-click a mob during it to drag it through the dimensions yourself (the same scene, with you doing the dragging); that ends the boost. Bosses and players just take the slash.

## Vergilius, the Red Gaze

As the Claw retreats, a folded page falls from his coat: **the Head's Hitlist**. "Limbus Company has grown teeth." One name can be read, Vergilius; the rest are blacked out for now. (Not from the rebuilt Claw, and not while you're still on a hitlist.)

Open it and it says where he was last seen (a few hundred blocks away, always on land). The trail has three stops, each built as you come near and found up close; the page updates after each:
1. **Burns**: three long scorch marks a white-hot blade left in the ground, still smoking, and what's left of whoever was in the way.
2. **Blood**: a trail of it, leading on.
3. **The place he waits**: a street lamp and a bench. He introduces himself ("You may call me, Vergilius.") and waits for your answer: **draw your weapon**, or **show him the hitlist**. Every block of the trail goes only into air or plants (nothing is broken) and is taken away again when it's over.

**Draw on him** and he fights: **Heated Puncture** (a lunge and a burning thrust), **Scorching Incision** (three burning cuts), **Following the Flow** (strike him in the stance and he turns it aside and answers), **I Shall Open the Path** (a line shows, he cuts through you along it, and a moment later the whole line splits open) and the **Red Gaze** (still in his sight when his eyes flare, you're slowed and weakened). At half health, his E.G.O, **Lavacrum Sanguinis**: the laurel crown, the pauldron, the blood cape, a lake of blood round him, and **Internal Bleeding** (spikes up under you, one after another), **Blinding Bloodcleaver**, **Drown in Blood** (dragged to the middle, then the whole lake spikes), **Blood Barrier** (the next three hits do nothing) and, below a quarter, **Funeral for a Dead Bloodfiend**. He doesn't die: brought down, he kneels, and the **Limbus bus** pulls up for him, opens its door, and drives him away. He leaves his **White-hot Gladius** and **his eyes** behind.

**Show him the hitlist** and the Claw comes back for you both, **rebuilt**: tearing his way in, eyes burning behind the mask, the claw polished new, bigger vials, more health, serums back sooner. Win, and Vergilius walks with you as a **companion**: he follows (and catches up from anywhere, other dimensions too), fights whatever you fight or whatever hits you (never players or your pets), kneels instead of dying and gets back up. Sneak + right-click him to have him wait or follow. One companion Vergilius per player. Turn on him (three blows meant for him within five seconds; sweeps that catch him don't count, and he warns you at the second) and he says what he thinks of that, and you fight him for the gladius and the eyes. Lose the rematch and he drives off; the hitlist leads back to him a little later.

**White-hot Gladius**: sets what it cuts on fire. **B** picks a skill (tap: next, hold: the wheel), right-click uses it: Heated Puncture, Scorching Incision, Following the Flow, I Shall Open the Path.

**His eyes**: worn in their own slot, not an armour slot. Use them, or click the eye slot in your inventory (above the shield slot) with them; click it with nothing in hand to take them out. Worn, your eyes glow red, and with the gladius in hand the wheel adds the **Red Gaze** and **Lavacrum Sanguinis**: a minute of E.G.O (the crown, pauldron and cape on you, a lake of blood that goes where you go) with the wheel turned to Internal Bleeding, Blinding Bloodcleaver, Drown in Blood and Funeral for a Dead Bloodfiend (costs four hearts, ends the E.G.O). They drop on death like anything else.

The Limbus bus is modelled after the game's own story art of it. Vergilius's skin by the user.

## E.G.O

| Item | How it works |
|---|---|
| Solemn Lament | Five coins. The Living and The Departed fire in turn at random enemies, each shot a swarm of funeral butterflies; hits heal you |
| Magic Bullet | One coin. The Freischutz fires through a corridor of magic circles; the count goes 1 to 7, and the seventh bullet is something else |
| 4th Match Flame | One coin. You appear before the enemy and write the character for "fierce" in fire, then it ignites |

E.G.O skills open with a Limbus-style cut-in in the sin's colour. Coins flip above your head; heads add power.

## Black Silence and Furioso
**Black Silence**: Roland's gloves from Library of Ruina, worn on both hands (black leather, the flat-lining pulse on the back). They go in the **Curios** hands slot (right-click to put them on), or in the offhand if Curios isn't installed.

**Learning them**: fresh off Roland they only half answer you: stuck on **Random mode** (a new weapon every few hits), no picking a weapon, no Furioso. Read **the Black Silence's Book** and win against the Claw that comes for it, and they answer you fully from then on (kept through death).

**The wheel**: tap **B** to switch to the next weapon, or hold **B** and point at one of his twelve, then let go. With an empty main hand, every attack uses that weapon:
- pistols and shotgun shoot (hitscan, and an 8-pellet cone)
- the lance thrusts and lunges
- the hammer smashes the ground around the target and briefly guards you
- the katana dashes through in one iai cut
- gauntlets jab, the knife bleeds, the axe sweeps, the mace launches
- the greatsword slams down hard
- the short swords cut twice with a sidestep
- Durandal makes a long cut

Holding a tool or block works as normal, and nothing touches the terrain. **Random mode** (click the wheel's centre) draws a new weapon every 3 hits, favouring ones you haven't used on that enemy yet.

**Furioso is earned**: land all twelve weapons on the same enemy (the pips right of the hotbar light up; progress resets after 30 s without hitting it, or if you switch targets). The wheel's centre then becomes **FURIOSO**, his page exactly as he plays it: Atelier Logic pistols, the Allas Workshop lance, the Old Boys Workshop hammer, the Mook Workshop katana, the Ranga Workshop gauntlets and combat knife, the Zelkova Workshop axe and mace, the Wheels Industry greatsword, the Crystal Atelier short swords, the Atelier Logic shotgun, and finally Durandal. Each weapon seeps out of the gloves in black mist, strikes as many times as its combat page has dice, and is tossed away to dissolve. Every big hit drops into slow motion with a steady camera shot, each new weapon gets a freeze-frame close-up with its workshop's name, and a hit counter climbs the whole way. The victim can't die before the last cut; anything that survives it bleeds (Wither) and is bound (Slowness).

The moves are keyframed in Blockbench: `tools/blockbench/furioso.bbmodel` (Animate tab, 1 s = 20 script ticks). After editing, run `python tools/bbmodel_to_furioso.py` to bake them into `FuriosoAnims.java`.

## Commands
`/projectminecraft cooldowns [players]` (operators): every cooldown of the mod's ready again for those players (or yourself): the gloves' weapons, the Handmade Claw's serums, the gladius's skills and its E.G.O skills, and the E.G.O weapons', book's and invitation's item cooldowns.

## Config
`projectminecraft-common.toml` (E.G.O cooldown multiplier; the gloves' damage multiplier (weapons and Furioso), how many hits Random mode takes to switch weapons, and Furioso's target rules (how long your target stays yours when you hit something else, or keeping the count across enemies altogether); the Claw's health, the dimensions his Serum W visits (any mod's dimension ids, missing ones are skipped) and how hard its slashes hit; Vergilius's health and damage, the rebuilt Claw's health, how far away the hitlist's trail starts) and `projectminecraft-client.toml` (screen shaders, camera shake, hit stop, impact frames, cinematics). Both are editable in-game through Mods > Project Minecraft > Config.

## Building
JDK 21 is required. `gradlew build` puts the jar in `build/libs/`.

Tours (dev-only visual checks, `src/tour`): delete `run-tour/saves/es_test`, then `gradlew runTourClient -DtourRoland=true` (the reception: invite, models, mask, arena, looks, p1-p5, death, reset) or `-DtourGloves=true` (every weapon held and swung, the model sheet, and a real Furioso) or `-DtourClaw=true` (summon, looks, each move, the dimension trip and its safety nets, retreat, losing, the Handmade Claw) or `-DtourVergilius=true` (looks, the hitlist and its trail, meeting him, each move, the E.G.O, the bus, the rematch, the companion, betrayal, the gladius and the eyes). `-DtourOnly=mask,furioso` keeps just the stops whose names contain one of those. Screenshots go to `run-tour/screenshots`.

Asset tools (`tools/`):
- `java tools/GenModels.java src/main/resources`: the E.G.O, Black Silence and Furioso weapon models (palette rows match Random Slop's sheets).
- `java tools/GenRolandTextures.java src/main/resources [skins|mist|distorted|beast]`: Angelica's hair, the Bygones, Angelica's mist, the distorted weapon textures and the beast (Roland's and Angelica's own skins are the user's art, not generated).
- `java tools/GenGloveTextures.java src/main/resources`: the gloves' worn texture and icon.
- `java tools/GenClawTextures.java src/main/resources`: the Claw's skin, his vials and plates, and the Claw item sprites.
- `java tools/GenVergiliusTextures.java src/main/resources <skin png> [skin|extras|items|bus]`: Vergilius's E.G.O skin, eye glow, crown/pauldron/cape/blades sheet, the item sprites and the Limbus bus. `python tools/gen_gladius_model.py`: the gladius's 3D item model. `python tools/build_verg_sounds.py`: his synthesized sounds and the rebuilt Claw's roar.
- `tools/gen_textures.ps1`: the item sprites. `tools/build_sounds.ps1` (ffmpeg): the E.G.O, Furioso and Claw sounds; the rest are copies of Random Slop's.
- `tools/blockbench/furioso.bbmodel` + `python tools/bbmodel_to_furioso.py`: Furioso's keyframes.
